About

About Calculators

Calculators is a small collection of free, fast, browser-based tools for everyday math — loans, savings, health, and general conversions. No signups, no downloads, no clutter. You put in a number, you get an answer.

Why we built this

Most calculator sites are slow, ad-heavy, or bury the actual tool under paragraphs of text. We wanted the opposite: open a page, see the calculator immediately, get your number, move on.

How the calculators work

Every calculation runs directly in your browser using standard, well-known formulas (EMI/loan amortization formulas, BMI/BMR formulas, compound interest formulas, and so on). Nothing you type is sent to a server — the math happens on your device.

Accuracy

We aim for accurate, formula-based results, but every calculator here is meant as an estimate to help with planning, not as financial, medical, or legal advice. See our Disclaimer for details.
