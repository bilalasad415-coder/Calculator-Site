Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how calculators24.online ("the site", "we", "us") handles information when you visit.

Calculator inputs

All calculators on this site run entirely in your browser. Numbers you enter (loan amount, weight, dates, etc.) are processed locally on your device and are not sent to, stored on, or seen by our servers.

Automatically collected information

Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited technical information through standard web technologies, which can include:

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on the site
  • Approximate location (country/region) based on IP address
  • Cookies used for basic site functionality and, if enabled, analytics or advertising

Third-party services

We use third-party services that may set their own cookies or collect data according to their own privacy policies, including:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (may log IP addresses per Google's standard practices)
  • Hosting provider (Netlify) — for serving the website
  • Google Analytics / Google AdSense (if and when enabled) — for site analytics and advertising, which may use cookies to personalize ads based on your visits to this and other sites

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Since we do not store calculator inputs or require accounts, there is generally no personal data tied to your calculator usage to request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
