India new tax regime — key points

USA federal brackets — 2026 (single filer)

Seven brackets from 10% to 37%, with a $16,100 standard deduction for single filers. Note: this covers federal tax only — state income tax is separate and varies (some states like Texas and Florida have none).

UK income tax — 2026/27

Personal Allowance of £12,570 is tax-free, then 20% basic rate to £50,270, 40% higher rate to £125,140, and 45% above that. This covers England, Wales & Northern Ireland rates — Scotland uses different bands. National Insurance is separate and not included here.
