How age is calculated precisely

This calculator finds the exact difference between two dates in years, months, and days — accounting for varying month lengths and leap years, which simple subtraction of birth year from current year doesn't handle correctly.

Why your "age in days" can be a fun number

Milestones like your 10,000th day of life often fall on unexpected dates — many people mark them as personal celebrations. The total-days figure here also makes it easy to compare ages precisely, like determining exact eligibility windows for age-restricted applications.

Common uses for exact age calculations

Exact age is often needed for school admission cutoffs, visa or exam eligibility, legal age verification, and calculating precise time until retirement or a specific milestone birthday.

Frequently asked questions

Does this account for leap years? Yes, the calculation is based on actual calendar dates, so leap years are automatically handled correctly.

Can I calculate age as of a future or past date, not just today? Yes — use the "Calculate age as of" field to set any reference date, useful for eligibility checks on a specific cutoff date.
